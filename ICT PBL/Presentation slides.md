# Slide 3 — Introduction

## Introduction

Finding parking spaces in crowded areas is often difficult and time-consuming. Many drivers waste fuel and time searching for available parking spots.

Our project focuses on creating a smart parking website that allows users to:

- Book parking spaces online
    
- View available slots in real time
    
- Use AI-powered license plate recognition
    
- Reduce traffic congestion and waiting time
    

This system helps make parking faster, easier, and more organized.

# Slide 4 — Problems We Identified

## Parking Problems in Daily Life

- Drivers spend too much time searching for parking.
    
- Traffic congestion increases near parking areas.
    
- Manual parking systems are slow and inefficient.
    
- Parking spaces are sometimes mismanaged.
    
- Lack of real-time parking information.
    
- Difficult to monitor vehicles manually.
    

These problems inspired us to create a smart parking solution.
