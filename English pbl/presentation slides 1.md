## **Slide 2: Introduction**

Many walking paths in Taungoo have very little shade.  
This makes walking uncomfortable, especially during hot weather.

Our project focuses on:

- Shade problems
    
- Environmental sustainability
    
- Community improvements
    

---

## **Slide 3: Driving Question (DQ)**

How can we, as Year 8 Taungoo students, use media and real-life actions to share solutions that help improve waste management, energy saving, air quality, and urban agriculture for a cleaner and more sustainable community?

---

## **Slide 4: Essential Question (EQ)**

How can we, as Year 8 Taungoo students, effectively raise awareness about lack of shade along walking paths and propose practical improvements that create a more comfortable, cooler community?

---

## **Slide 5: Problem Statement**

- Few trees along walking routes
    
- High temperatures in walking areas
    
- Lack of public shade structures
    
- Reduced comfort and health risks
    

---

## **Slide 6: Issue Objectives**

We aim to:

- Identify shade problems in Taungoo
    
- Understand public opinions
    
- Suggest practical solutions
    
- Promote awareness in the community
    

---

## **Slide 7: Aim of the Project**

To create awareness and suggest solutions that improve shade, reduce heat, and support a greener environment in Taungoo.

---

## **Slide 8: Expected Effects**

If implemented:

- Cooler walking areas
    
- Improved comfort for pedestrians
    
- Better air quality
    
- Greener urban environment
    

---

## **Slide 9: Survey Overview**

We conducted a survey with students and local people to understand:

- Heat comfort levels
    
- Availability of shade
    
- Preferred solutions
    

---

## **Slide 10: Survey Questions**

We asked:

- Do you feel walking paths are too hot?
    
- Is there enough shade in your area?
    
- What solutions do you prefer?
    
- Should trees or shelters be added?
    

---

## **Slide 11: Interview Questions**

We asked local/community members:

- Where do you feel the hottest when walking?
    
- What improvements would you like to see?
    
- Do you support planting more trees or building shade structures?
    

---

## **Slide 12: Survey Results Summary**

- Most people said **walking paths are too hot**
    
- Very few shaded areas exist
    
- Majority prefer **trees + solar shade structures**
    
- Strong support for urban greening
    

---

## **Slide 13: Key Findings**

- Shade is urgently needed
    
- Trees are the most preferred solution
    
- People want both natural and built shade
    
- Awareness is still low in some areas
    

---

## **Slide 14: Solution 1 – Plant More Trees**

- Plant native trees along roads
    
- Create green walking corridors
    
- Trees provide natural cooling and air purification
    

---

## **Slide 15: Solution 2 – Solar Shade Structures**

- Install solar panel roofs over walkways
    
- Provide shade and produce electricity
    
- Eco-friendly dual-purpose system
    

---

## **Slide 16: Solution 3 – Green Roofs & Vertical Gardens**

- Add plants on walls and rooftops
    
- Reduce heat in urban areas
    
- Improve air quality
    

---

## **Slide 17: Solution 4 – Smart Irrigation Systems**

- Use sensors to water plants efficiently
    
- Prevent water waste
    
- Keep trees and plants healthy
