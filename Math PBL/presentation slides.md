# Slide 7 — How is Electricity Produced?

## Electricity Generation Process

- Electricity is produced using generators and turbines.
    
- Moving water, steam, air, or gases spin turbines.
    
- Generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.
    
- Electricity is then transferred through power lines to homes and buildings.

# Slide 10 — Types of Solar Energy

## Types of Solar Energy

### Photovoltaic Solar Energy

- Converts sunlight directly into electricity using solar panels.
    

### Thermal Solar Energy

- Uses solar heat to warm water or produce steam.
    

### Passive Solar Energy

- Uses building design and sunlight naturally without machines.
    

### Hybrid Solar Energy

- Combines solar energy with other renewable sources like wind.

# Slide 14 — Can a City Rely Only on Solar Energy?

## Can Solar Power Run a City?

### Advantages

- Reduces pollution and carbon emissions
    
- Provides renewable energy
    
- Lowers long-term electricity costs
    
- Improves energy security
    

### Challenges

- Solar panels only work during sunny periods
    
- Energy storage systems are needed
    
- High installation costs
    
- Large infrastructure is required
    

Conclusion:  
Cities may not rely only on solar energy yet, but solar can become a major energy source when combined with batteries and other renewable energy systems.
